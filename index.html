iCrack

Crack vaults with the hinge of your iPhone Duo.

How to play

The hinge is the dial. Fold slowly: you feel a soft tick on every number and a heavy clunk on the right one. Tap the dial to lock the number in, then reverse direction for the next one. When you hear footsteps, close your iPhone Duo and wait until the guard has walked past on the cover screen.

Which devices?

iCrack is made for iPhone Duo. The game needs the hinge, so it does not run on other iPhones.
